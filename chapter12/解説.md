# 解説

自動で`Machine`のメンバである`engine_`のデストラクタが走ることがポイント。
`Machine()`のコンストラクタが走る前にMachineのメンバ(今回の場合`Engine_`)は作成される。
`Machine`は作成されないので`Machine`のコンストラクタは走らない。



[Constructors and member initializer lists](https://en.cppreference.com/cpp/language/constructor)


[class.base.init](https://eel.is/c++draft/class.base.init)

`regardless of the order of the mem-initializers` と書かれている

抜粋
>In a non-delegating constructor, initialization proceeds in the following order:
>(15.1)
> First, and only for the constructor of the most derived class ([intro.object]), virtual base classes are initialized in the order they appear on a depth-first left-to-right traversal of the directed acyclic graph of base classes, where “left-to-right” is the order of appearance of the base classes in the derived class base-specifier-list.
> (15.2)
> Then, direct base classes are initialized in declaration order as they appear in the base-specifier-list (regardless of the order of the mem-initializers).
> (15.3)
> Then, non-static data members are initialized in the order they were declared in the class definition (again regardless of the order of the mem-initializers).
> (15.4)
> Finally, the compound-statement of the constructor body is executed.
